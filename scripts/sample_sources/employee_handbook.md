# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Version 4.2, effective 1 March 2026. Owned by the People Operations team.

## 1. About Northwind Labs

Northwind Labs builds Orbit Analytics, a product analytics platform used by more than 1,400 software companies. The company was founded in 2019 in Pune, India, and now has 312 employees across offices in Pune, Bengaluru and Berlin, plus a fully remote cohort in eleven countries.

Our three operating principles are: write things down, default to trust, and ship small. Every policy in this handbook should be read in the spirit of those principles. When a situation is not covered here, ask your manager or post in the #ask-people channel.

## 2. Working Hours and Remote Work

Northwind Labs follows a hybrid-first model. Employees attached to an office are expected on-site on Tuesdays and Thursdays, which are our collaboration days. The remaining days can be worked from anywhere within the employee's country of employment.

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendee's agreement.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 calendar days per year, and requires approval from both your manager and the People Operations team at least 14 days in advance. This limit exists because of tax and immigration rules, so exceptions are rare and need sign-off from the Head of People.

Fully remote employees receive a one-time home-office stipend of INR 60,000 (or EUR 700 for employees in Europe) and a monthly internet allowance of INR 2,000 (EUR 40).

## 3. Leave Policy

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 8 unused days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March. Leave requests of 5 or more consecutive working days should be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance through the HR portal (PeopleHub).

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required only for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

### 3.3 Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents, including adoptive parents, receive 12 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first 12 months after the child's arrival. Parents returning from parental leave may work a 4-day week at full pay for their first 4 weeks back.

### 3.4 Recharge Week

The whole company closes for a "Recharge Week" during the last week of December. Recharge Week does not count against annual leave. On-call engineers who work during Recharge Week receive equivalent days off in January.

## 4. Expenses and Travel

Expenses must be submitted in PeopleHub within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemised receipt. Expenses below INR 5,000 (EUR 60) are auto-approved; anything above needs manager approval. Purchases above INR 50,000 (EUR 600) require pre-approval before the money is spent.

Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 75,000 (EUR 900) for books, courses and conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over.

For business travel, economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. The daily meal allowance during travel is INR 3,000 in India and EUR 60 elsewhere. Travel must be booked through the company travel desk (TravelDesk) unless booking directly is at least 15% cheaper.

## 5. Security and Acceptable Use

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and the endpoint agent (Sentinel) running. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every company system. Hardware security keys are required for engineers with production access and for everyone in Finance.

Customer data must never be copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage, or public AI tools. Approved AI assistants are listed on the Security wiki page; using any other AI tool with company or customer data is a policy violation.

Suspected security incidents, including lost devices and phishing emails that were clicked, must be reported to security@northwindlabs.example within 1 hour of discovery. Reporting quickly is never penalised; hiding an incident is.

## 6. Performance and Growth

Performance conversations happen twice a year, in April and October. Each cycle includes a self-review, peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager review. Compensation is reviewed once a year in the April cycle.

Engineering career levels run from L1 (Associate Engineer) to L7 (Principal Engineer). Promotions up to L4 are decided by a calibration committee of the engineering managers; promotions to L5 and above also need approval from the CTO.

## 7. Benefits

Northwind Labs provides group health insurance covering the employee, spouse or partner, children and up to two parents, with a sum insured of INR 10 lakh per family. Employees in Germany are covered by statutory health insurance plus a supplementary dental plan.

The company matches pension or provident-fund contributions up to 6% of base salary. Every employee also receives an annual wellness allowance of INR 18,000 (EUR 250), usable for gym memberships, therapy, sports equipment or fitness apps.

## 8. Leaving Northwind Labs

The notice period is 30 days for levels L1 to L3 and 60 days for L4 and above. Company equipment must be returned within 7 days of the last working day. Exit interviews are optional and are run by People Operations, not by the departing employee's manager.
